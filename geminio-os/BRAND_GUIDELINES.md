# Geminio OS Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea
Geminio OS is an intelligent workspace that helps people move from intention to action. The identity should feel capable, calm, precise, premium, and trustworthy.

## Primary mark
The symbol is a continuous geometric G with a small orbit node. The G represents Geminio; the orbit represents connected tools, context, and intelligent movement. Vector source: assets/brand/geminio-mark.svg.

## Wordmark
Use assets/brand/geminio-wordmark.svg on dark backgrounds. In product UI, use the reusable BrandMark component.

Shared theme tokens remain authoritative for semantic success, warning, error, and focus colors.

## Typography
Use the platform system sans family. Keep hierarchy clear, line-height readable, and letter-spaced uppercase labels short.

## Logo rules
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space.
- Do not add outlines, shadows, or extra symbols to the primary mark.
- Use the mark alone in compact and loading contexts.
- Maintain strong contrast against the selected surface.
- Do not place the full wordmark inside small launcher icons.

## Motion
The loading mark uses a restrained pulse and orbit. Motion is a progress affordance, not decoration. Avoid flashing or rapid rotation.

## Welcome screen layout
- Top row: compact brand lockup left; workspace descriptor right.
- Hero: centered mark, eyebrow, headline, and short explanation.
- Value propositions: three concise rows.
- Primary action: full-width indigo button near the bottom.
- Secondary sign-in action: outlined button directly below.
- Privacy note: quiet text below both actions.
- Phone: single column and safe-area padding. Wider screens: content capped at 560 px.

## Interaction behavior
- Get started opens the existing sign-up flow.
- Existing-account action opens the existing sign-in flow.
- Buttons provide pressed feedback and accessible labels.
- App initialization displays the branded loading screen.
- Native launch surface uses the midnight background to avoid a white flash.
